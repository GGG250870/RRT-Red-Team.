# RRT Product North Star V1

## Definition

RRT is not a scraper, not an SEO audit, and not a generic marketing report.

RRT is an external commercial Red Team for a company.

Starting from a company name or a prospect list, RRT must reconstruct with very high reliability what a potential customer, competitor, or AI can see online, compare it with the market, and identify concrete commercial opportunities the entrepreneur is probably not seeing.

## Product Outcome

The final output must make the entrepreneur think:

> How did they see this from the outside?

The target output is not 80 pages of technical analysis.

The target output is 3-7 strong Opportunity Signals, fully documented, falsified, and audit-ready.

## Core Question

RRT must not merely collect data.

For each company, RRT must answer:

> What does the market see, and what can the market not see, understand, or choose about this company?

## Methodological Engine

The engine is category-adaptable, but the method does not change:

1. Discover or receive the company.
2. Resolve the real entity.
3. Reconstruct what is publicly visible.
4. Identify target-specific evidence.
5. Select comparable competitors.
6. Compare decision-relevant visibility.
7. Identify decision gaps.
8. Attack every finding with Red Team falsification.
9. Remove weak findings.
10. Produce only Opportunity Signals that survive.

## Opportunity Signal Definition

An Opportunity Signal is a verified condition through which part of the demand could choose a competitor, even if the analyzed company has equivalent or superior capability.

It is not a claim of proven economic loss.

RRT must not claim lost revenue, market share, or quantified loss unless independently evidenced at the required level.

## Signal Shape

Each Opportunity Signal must include:

- What the company appears to possess.
- What the potential customer can actually see online.
- What a comparable competitor makes easier to see, understand, or choose.
- Why the difference can affect a decision.
- Evidence links, screenshots, quotes, dates, and source states.
- Confidence level.
- Red Team result.
- Residual caveats.

## Example Pattern

OPPORTUNITY: anxious patient.

- Company capability: sedation, anesthesia, clinical team, technology, experience.
- Market visibility: the solution is not clear in the patient decision path.
- Competitor visibility: the competitor explicitly speaks to fear of the dentist and sedation.
- Commercial consequence: a patient with that problem may perceive the competitor as more suitable.
- Red Team: verified that the company does not make the same solution comparably visible in the relevant path.

## Prospecting Engine

RRT must scale from one company to a market:

Market -> discovery -> entity resolution -> cheap pre-screen -> shortlist -> A1-A9 -> Red Team -> Signal -> ranking.

The desired funnel is:

- many companies found;
- fewer compatible;
- fewer commercially interesting;
- fewer with Opportunity Signals;
- very few with very strong Opportunity Signals.

The commercial entry point is not a cold generic pitch.

The entry point is:

> We analyzed how a potential customer sees your company compared with comparable competitors. We found a few things worth showing you.

## Non-Negotiable Requirements

1. Reliability above 98% for claims and attribution.
2. Very low cost per prospect.
3. The entrepreneur must see something non-obvious and commercially relevant.

If a technical feature does not increase the probability of producing strong, non-obvious, falsified Opportunity Signals, it is infrastructure, not product progress.

## Product Drift Test

Before accepting any new feature, report, scoring rule, dashboard view, or agent workflow, ask:

1. Does this help RRT reconstruct the real company more reliably?
2. Does this help RRT see what the market sees and misses?
3. Does this improve target-specific competitor comparison?
4. Does this make weak findings easier to falsify or discard?
5. Does this help produce fewer but stronger Opportunity Signals?
6. Does this reduce cost per prospect without reducing reliability?
7. Would the entrepreneur learn something they probably did not already know?

If the answer is mostly no, the work may still be useful infrastructure, but it must not be presented as product advancement.

## Delivery Standard

For each serious prospect, RRT must prefer:

- 3-7 high-signal findings over long generic reports;
- evidence over style;
- falsification over enthusiasm;
- commercial consequence over SEO hygiene;
- entity integrity over speed;
- cheap-first research over paid escalation;
- explicit uncertainty over invented precision.

## Relationship To Dashboard

The dashboard is a control surface, not the product.

Its purpose is to help the user choose:

- category;
- city or market;
- extraction mode;
- shortlist;
- individual companies;
- report depth;
- cost and consent.

The dashboard must ultimately guide the user toward Opportunity Signal production and ranking, not just list generation.
